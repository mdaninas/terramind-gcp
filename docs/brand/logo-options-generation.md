# Terramind logo options

The Neural Cube option is the selected [project identity](README.md).

Generation method: built-in image generation, with transparent background enabled.
All three options are 2172 x 724 RGBA PNG files. PNG integrity and transparent
pixels were verified. The dark wordmarks are intended for light backgrounds.

- [Modular T](terramind-option-1-modular-t.png): a tiled T using Terraform-inspired geometry.
- [TM Monogram](terramind-option-2-tm-monogram.png): interlocking initials with violet and teal facets.
- [Neural Cube](terramind-option-3-neural-cube.png): a faceted cube with three connected nodes.

Design basis: the Terraform-inspired isometric tile language from the original
[logo generation](logo-generation.md). No reference images were supplied to the
generator.

## Final generation prompts

### Modular T

```text
Use case: logo-brand.
Asset type: logo option for Terramind, a developer tool that analyzes proposed Terraform infrastructure changes for risk and explains findings with grounded AI.
Style: professional, minimal, precise flat vector-style graphic with crisp smooth edges and large simple shapes. Use Terraform's modular isometric parallelogram construction and violet architectural geometry as the visual basis, adapted into a distinctive Terramind mark.
Layout: one horizontal logo, compact symbol on the left, exact wordmark "Terramind" on the right, optically aligned and carefully kerned. Wide 3:1 composition with balanced clear space. Dark green charcoal #142E32 modern geometric sans-serif wordmark, semibold, capital T and the remainder lowercase. Main icon colors rich violet #7B42BC and deep teal #00645D, with a slightly lighter violet only where a separate flat facet helps define structure.
Backdrop: genuine transparent alpha background for use on white or pale backgrounds.
Constraints: a single finished logo option, no labels or option numbers inside the image, no board of alternatives, no mockup, no subtitle or slogan, no extra text, no HashiCorp name, no Terraform wordmark, no gradients, no shadows, no glow, no texture, no bevels, no 3D rendered effects, no watermark, and no fake checkerboard. Legible at small sizes.
Concept: MODULAR T. Make this the clearest, simplest and most architectural option. Build a bold capital T from four to five separated isometric parallelogram tiles with Terraform-like 30-degree diagonals and uniform narrow gaps. A strong top crossbar and a clearly descending vertical stem are essential. Use violet for the crossbar and upper stem and a restrained teal tile at the base. Keep all tile edges and angles consistent. No circuit lines, no dots, no extra emblem. The symbol should immediately read as a T made of infrastructure building blocks.
```
