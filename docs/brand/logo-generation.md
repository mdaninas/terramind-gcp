# Initial Terramind logo generation

Asset: [terramind-logo-initial-concept.png](terramind-logo-initial-concept.png)

This initial concept is retained for reference. The selected project identity is
the [Neural Cube](README.md).

The logo uses purple isometric tile geometry, connected nodes and a teal accent.
The wordmark reads "Terramind". The file is a 2172 x 724 RGBA PNG with verified
transparent pixels, intended for light backgrounds.

Generation method: built-in image generation, with transparent background enabled.
This is raster artwork.

Design reference: [Terraform product logos](https://www.hashicorp.com/en/brand/hcp-product-logos).
The reference informed the generation prompt; no reference image was supplied to
the generator.

## Final generation prompt

```text
Use case: logo-brand
Asset type: primary logo for Terramind, a Terraform change intelligence project that analyzes proposed Terraform changes for infrastructure risk and provides grounded AI explanations.
Primary request: Generate one polished, production-ready Terramind logo using the Terraform logo as the visual basis. Borrow its recognizable design language of purple modular isometric parallelogram tiles, crisp angles, deliberate gaps and architectural structure. Develop a distinctive Terramind T symbol with a compact top crossbar and descending stem. Integrate a restrained intelligence motif through two or three simple connected nodes or negative-space branches within the tiled construction, with no literal brain illustration. The result should read first as a strong geometric T, then as connected infrastructure intelligence.
Style/medium: precise flat vector-style logo artwork, clean solid color fills, exceptionally crisp smooth edges, professional developer-tool identity, visually balanced and legible at small sizes.
Composition/framing: a single horizontal logo lockup, geometric symbol on the left and the exact wordmark "Terramind" on the right. Align the symbol optically with the wordmark. The symbol is moderately taller than the letters, with generous clear space and a carefully measured gap. Center the entire lockup in a wide canvas, with practical but not excessive transparent margins. Do not show multiple alternatives, a presentation board, or a mockup.
Color palette: Terraform-inspired rich violet for the main geometric tiles, a restrained deep teal #00645D accent on one secondary tile or node to match Terramind's current interface, and dark green charcoal #142E32 for the wordmark. Use solid colors with only flat facet color changes.
Text (verbatim): "Terramind". Capital T followed by lowercase erramind. Use a modern well-spaced geometric sans-serif, medium to semibold, careful kerning, a clear lowercase r and distinct m.
Scene/backdrop: truly transparent background with alpha channel, suitable for placing on white or pale backgrounds.
Constraints: one original cohesive logo asset; keep the visual relationship to Terraform's modular isometric T geometry obvious while giving Terramind its own identifiable construction. No HashiCorp name, no Terraform wordmark, no slogans, no subtitle, no extra labels. No gradient, shadow, glow, bevel, texture, 3D rendering, device mockup, border, decorative background, watermark, or fake transparency checkerboard. Avoid tiny circuit details or generic AI sparkle.
```
